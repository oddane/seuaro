# Code of Conduct

Seuaro is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want participation to be professional, welcoming and free of harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be precise and honest, especially about security, risk and test results.
- Give credit, including to upstream projects and prior work.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Promoting tokens, investment schemes, or price speculation in project spaces.
- Disclosing unpatched vulnerabilities publicly instead of through SECURITY.md.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, events) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement
Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the contact in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, in proportion to the behavior. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from their role.
